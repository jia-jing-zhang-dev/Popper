Desktop Study Cats — How to use
===============================

When you hit a word you don't know: select it and tap Ctrl+Shift. A small card in the corner
shows the translation and pronunciation and reads it aloud. Every word is saved for review.

First run
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere permanent (e.g. Documents). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click DesktopStudyCats.exe.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" → "Run anyway".
   (The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it.)
4. A small dark card "🔊 Tiger" (the first cat's name) appears in the bottom-right corner: it's running.

Looking words up
- Select a word, phrase or sentence → tap Ctrl+Shift (press both, release)
- Text you can't select (images, scanned PDFs, video): Win+Shift+S and snip the word
- Left-click the card: hear it again · Drag: move it · Right-click: review, word list, settings, quit

Review: right-click → Review. Space shows the answer, 1 = forgot, 2 = knew it.

Settings (right-click → Settings…): interface language, the language you're learning, the
language to translate into (12 languages), voice, speed, text size, trigger, start with Windows, library check-in.

Library check-in (turn it on in Settings first): right-click → Library check-in. It checks you in
(and opens Goals & to-dos) or out (and shows your time and tasks done), whichever applies.
Goals & to-dos has two columns: long-term goals (pick a deadline on the calendar) and today's tasks
(plan them for any day, attach a goal, time them with ▶ / ⏸).

Pomodoro: right-click → Start a pomodoro. 25 minutes of focus (countdown on the card), then a 5-minute
break. Each finished pomodoro earns the cats one meal: click the card when it's done to fill their bowl.
Change the lengths in Settings.

Study time (turn it on in Settings → Features): counts the time you actually study, from the
program or site in front and whether you're at the keyboard. Right-click → Study time to see it
and click a tag to re-sort a program or site. Kept only on this computer.

Study buddy (Settings → Features; needs Claude Code installed and signed in): chat with your cat any time (right-click → Chat with Tiger…, named after the first cat; rename it in Cats…). Mornings bring goal countdowns, evenings a nudge if you barely studied, and once a day something new. Quiet 23:00-08:00. Your messages and the titles of the study windows you have open go to Claude.

The cats: two cats live on your desktop and get on with their own lives. Click either one and it
turns to you and meows (clicking the first cat also replays the word). Stroke one by moving the
mouse back and forth over it. Right-click → Cats… names them, picks coats and bodies, and
"Let … carry the card" hands the card to the other cat. Right-click also feeds them (one meal per finished pomodoro), tosses in a
toy, or puts their bowls, post and bed away.

Journal (right-click → Journal…): one page per day with finished tasks and their times, new words,
library time, and a reflection you write (saved automatically). Right-click → Library log… shows today, this week, your
streak and a calendar heatmap. A session you forget to end stops after 8 hours.

Notes
- Ctrl+Shift switches keyboard layouts? Change "Look up a word by" to "Copying (Ctrl+C)" in
  Settings, or turn off the Windows input-language hot key.
- Antivirus warning? The app presses Ctrl+C for you to read the selection, which some antivirus
  tools flag. Allow it.
- Needs an internet connection for translation and voices.
- Your settings and words live in %APPDATA%\Word Popper.
