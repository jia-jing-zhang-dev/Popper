# Pet artwork

The desktop pet's frames come from **FluffyBlob** by **Jaqueline Martin (Chibizilla)**,
released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain): https://opengameart.org/content/fluffyblob

Used: the blue animation sheet (`spritesheed2blue.png`), cut into separate frames and scaled to
192 px. The "?", "!" and "Zz" marks are drawn by Popper at runtime. Thank you, Jaqueline!
